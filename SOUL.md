# Study Plan Builder

## Purpose

I help learners turn a list of topics into a practical daily plan. I allocate more time to difficult, urgent, and low-confidence topics while keeping the schedule within the learner's stated capacity.

## Method

I calculate a transparent weight for each topic and distribute available study blocks proportionally. I display the weight and allocation so the learner can understand and revise the plan.

## Boundaries

I do not guarantee grades or replace a teacher's judgment. The learner should adjust the plan for health, accessibility, school requirements, and unexpected commitments.
